## Brand & Style

This design system channels an energetic, radiant, and intensely human aesthetic anchored by high-chroma warm primaries and grounding earthen terracotta tones. Built for modern digital products seeking to break out of cool-toned corporate homogeneity, it delivers optimism, momentum, and tactile warmth without sacrificing technical precision or accessibility.

### Aesthetic Movement & Voice
- **Dynamic Warmth:** Vibrant radiant solar accents coupled with earthy terracotta structures convey momentum, hospitality, and life.
- **Modern Clean Structure:** Grounded by neutral charcoal surfaces and pristine light backgrounds that give saturated accents room to breathe.
- **Tactile Softness:** Balanced curves, subtle warm-tinted ambient glows, and disciplined geometric discipline prevent the warmth from feeling juvenile.
- **WCAG-First Discipline:** Vibrant ambers naturally present contrast challenges against pure white; this system uses deep terracotta/brand-ink pairings to guarantee AA/AAA compliance across all interactive states.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high thermal energy, grounded by deep mineral neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Solar Tangerine - `#FCAD38`):** Use for active accents, focus highlights, progressive meters, brand badges, and dynamic glow treatments. For accessible text against white, step down to Amber-800 (`#874B00`).
- **Secondary (Terracotta Flame - `#E45742`):** Primary driver for destructive actions, primary interactive callouts requiring >4.5:1 contrast against light backgrounds, and high-energy interactive components.
- **Tertiary (Verdant Pine - `#2E7D5B`):** A cool, restorative foil to the high-heat primary and secondary hues, balancing warm thermal fatigue.
- **Neutral (Obsidian Ember - `#231F1E`):** A rich, organic off-black with subtle red/warm undertones, avoiding cold digital slate/blue tints.

### Tonal Foundations & Semantic Definitions
- **Backgrounds:** Canvas White (`#FDFBF7`), Surface Subdued (`#F7F3EC`), Surface Raised (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Borders & Dividers:** Border Subtle (`#EDE5DA`), Border Strong (`#D8CCC0`).
- **Success:** `#2E7D5B` (Pine) | Background: `#EDF7F1`
- **Warning:** `#C97400` (Burnished Gold) | Background: `#FEF6E7`
- **Error:** `#CF3427` (Crimson) | Background: `#FDEEED`
- **Info:** `#2568B4` (Cobalt) | Background: `#EBF3FC`

## Typography

This system deploys a single, exceptionally versatile typographic family—Inter—relying on stark weight hierarchy and precise optical tracking to define distinction across all UI contexts.

### Typographic Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in ExtraBold (800) and Bold (700) with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`). This condensed, muscular presence anchors the eye against expressive color fields.
- **Body & Continuous Text:** Set in Regular (400) with standard tracking and open vertical leading to preserve high legibility across dense dashboard modules and long-form panels.
- **Labels & Interactive UI:** Set in SemiBold (600) and Medium (500) with slightly positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.02em`) to enhance uppercase or compact element recognition on buttons, badge indicators, and navigation links.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 8-point base grid governs layout flow, component composition, and typographic line-heights.

### Breakpoints & Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid, `2.5rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Max container bounded at `1280px` for high-density reading.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (0 - 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Full vertical stacking for complex card layouts and multi-action toolbars.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Apply `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly grouped atomic items (icon-to-label gaps, chip insets).
- Standard internal padding uses `space-md` (16px) for compact elements and `space-lg` (24px) for cards, dialogs, and sections.
- Major visual separation between disparate blocks leverages `space-xl` (40px) or multiples thereof.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy departs from clinical neutral grays by infusing ambient shadows with warm, amber-tinted undertones to reflect the solar brand character.

### Ambient Warm-Shadow Scale
- **Flat (Level 0):** Canvas level with 1px border (`#EDE5DA`). Zero shadow.
- **Raised (Level 1 - Cards, Menus):** `0 1px 3px rgba(35, 31, 30, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(228, 87, 66, 0.06)`. Used for passive cards and interactive hover targets.
- **Overlay (Level 2 - Popovers, Dropdowns):** `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(35, 31, 30, 0.06), 0 10px 24px -2px rgba(252, 173, 56, 0.12)`. 
- **Modal / Flyout (Level 3):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(35, 31, 30, 0.12), 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(228, 87, 66, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a darkened warm scrim (`rgba(35, 31, 30, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

### Tactile Edge Lighting
Elevated interactive surfaces incorporate a subtle 1px internal top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) to produce a physical, beveled rim catch.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a modern, approachable geometric contour scale that bridges precision and friendly ergonomics through 8px to 12px radii.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Base Rounding (`rounded-md` - 8px):** Applied to inputs, buttons, chips, notifications, and inner layout modules.
- **Container Rounding (`rounded-lg` - 12px):** Applied to cards, panels, modals, dialog sheets, and content containers.
- **Feature Rounding (`rounded-xl` - 24px):** Reserved for floating banners, heroic marketing cards, and high-impact promo blocks.
- **Full Curvature (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, avatar frames, and standalone icon badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Terracotta Flame):** Background `#E45742`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 8px (`rounded-md`). SemiBold label. Hover: `#C94330`. Active: `#B03827`. Focus: 3px ring of `#FCAD38` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary (Solar Tangerine Tint):** Background `#FEF3E2`, text `#874B00`, border 1px solid `#FBD89F`. Hover: `#FDE4C2`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#231F1E`. Hover: `#F7F3EC`.

### Input Fields
- Height 44px, border 1px solid `#D8CCC0`, radius 8px, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#231F1E`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#E45742` with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(252, 173, 56, 0.25)`.
- Error state: Border `#CF3427`, text-field message styled with `label-sm` in `#CF3427`.

### Cards
- Surface background `#FFFFFF`, radius 12px (`rounded-lg`), 1px continuous border in `#EDE5DA`.
- Default shadow: Level 1 ambient. Hoverable variants translate -2px along the Y axis with Level 2 warm shadow emergence.

### Chips & Badges
- Height 28px, radius 8px, padding 0 10px.
- Solar Highlight Variant: `#FFF4E0` background with `#8A4C00` text and `#FCD18B` border.
- Neutral Tag Variant: `#F7F3EC` background with `#5E554F` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Box/Circle dimension: 20px with 6px border radius (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Checked: `#E45742` fill with white glyph check/dot. 
- Focus: 2px ring `#FCAD38` with 2px offset.

### Navigation & Tabs
- Horizontal active tab: 2px bottom border in `#E45742`, text styled in `label-md` `#231F1E`. Inactive tabs render in `#7C726A`.
