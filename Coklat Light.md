## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an organic, quiet-luxury modernism centered around warmth, permanence, and calm intentionality. Merging the tactile sensibilities of natural architecture with pristine digital execution, it rejects both the sterility of clinical corporate interfaces and the hyper-vibrant noise of contemporary consumer applications. 

### Visual Philosophy
- **Natural Tactility:** Digital components evoke raw terracotta, stone, milled timber, and sun-bleached botanicals.
- **Architectural Composition:** Generous internal whitespace, purposeful structural boundaries, and disciplined content framing anchor every interaction.
- **Calm Authority:** Understated typography balanced against deeply rich, earthy tones creates an interface that feels steady, durable, and respectful of user attention.

### Audience & Mood
Engineered for products across architectural tools, sustainable commerce, natural wellness, editorial publishing, and grounded financial platforms. The interface evokes stability, thoughtful focus, tactile craftsmanship, and enduring elegance.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from physical minerals, clay strata, and Mediterranean foliage, engineered into accessible digital tokens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#8A5F41` — Earth Clay):** Deep roasted loam and warm terracotta. Used for critical actions, key landmarks, active interactive states, and foundational branding accents.
- **Secondary (`#A5AF79` — Olive Sage):** Subdued, powdery botanical green. Used to communicate secondary highlights, balance indicators, success states, subtle badges, and soft focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#D88B57` — Burnt Ochre):** A sunlit warmth used sparingly for subtle calls to attention, notifications, energetic highlights, and accent chips.
- **Neutral (`#76746F` — Milled Silt):** A calibrated warm stone tone that anchors typography, borders, and contextual surfaces without introducing cold blue casts.

### Surface System (Light Mode Dominant)
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF9F6` (Sun-dried linen paper)
- **Surface Level 1:** `#F5F2ED` (Warm plaster)
- **Surface Level 2:** `#EEEAE2` (Milled travertine)
- **Surface Level 3:** `#E4DFC8` (Pressed sandstone)
- **On-Surface High Emphasis:** `#2C2621` (Charred peat)
- **On-Surface Medium Emphasis:** `#5E5953` (Weathered stone)
- **Border Dim:** `#E5DFD6` (Subtle boundary)
- **Border Default:** `#D5CFC5` (Structural delimiter)

## Typography

Inter serves as the singular typographic voice throughout this design system. By pairing neutral, hyper-legible neo-grotesque structural letterforms with tight tracking and calibrated vertical rhythms, the typography creates a structured editorial poise that anchors the organic palette.

### Typographic Guidelines
- **Optical Kerning & Tight Tracking:** For sizes at or above 24px (`headline-md`), negative letter spacing between `-0.015em` and `-0.03em` is mandatory to give Inter a structured, high-end editorial tension.
- **Micro Labels:** Functional badges, tracking tags, and small meta chips leverage `label-sm` with a `+0.04em` letter spacing boost in sentence or uppercase styling to preserve crisp readability against earth-toned backgrounds.
- **Reading Length:** Body text must strictly stay capped between 45 and 70 characters per line, utilizing the comfortable line-height ratios defined in `body-lg` and `body-xl`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes airy, architectural containment built on an underlying 8pt spatial grid with a 4pt sub-grid for tight inline items.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded at 1440px max-width. Columns employ `margin: 3rem` outer padding and `gutter: 1.5rem` between modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-sm: 1.25rem` outer edge safety and `gutter-sm: 1rem`.

### Spatial Flow
Whitespace is treated as a positive physical presence rather than empty room. Vertical rhythm preserves distinct groupings: component internals adhere strictly to `space-xs` through `space-md`, card bodies maintain `space-lg`, and major view sections breathe across `space-xl` (40px) or multi-token composites (64px/80px) on desktop layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids synthetic, weightless shadows or high-altitude drop-shadows. Instead, depth is articulated through layered physical material planes, warm-tinted ambient occlusion, and subtle low-contrast architectural borders.

### The Depth Matrix
1. **Tonal Planar Stacking:** Surfaces step forward by shifting from warm canvas base (`#FBF9F6`) up to pure elevated plaster surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCFA`). The eye perceives depth through clean step-changes in value rather than heavy elevation blur.
2. **Warm Silt Ambient Shadows:** When an element floats (modals, dropdowns, sticky action bars), shadows use a warm umber tint (`rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.06)` to `rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.12)`) with broad dispersion:
   - *Low / Card Rest:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.05), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.03)`
   - *Mid / Card Hover & Menus:* `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.04)`
   - *High / Overlays & Drawers:* `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.14), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(44, 38, 33, 0.05)`
3. **Hairline Boundaries:** Floating surfaces are framed with a hairline perimeter (`1px solid #E5DFD6` or 50% opacity translucent tint) to simulate the clean edge of honed stone.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is set to **Rounded (Level 2)**, conveying modern, polished organic surfaces akin to smoothed pebble riverstones, carved ceramics, and softened clayware.

### Corner Radii Scale
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem - 8px):** Buttons, text input fields, tooltips, tags, and small dropdown triggers.
- **Containers & Panels (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem - 16px):** Content cards, dialog boxes, image viewports, and table enclosures.
- **Hero & Outer Shells (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem - 24px):** Primary hero banners, persistent flyout drawers, and segmented layout shells.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, chips, and compact pill-button variants.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled background in earth brown (`#8A5F41`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded` (8px). Height: 44px (touch-compliant). Hover: `#774F34` with subtle `-1px` y-axis translation. Active: `#634027`. Focus: ring of 3px sage tint (`#A5AF79` at 40% opacity).
- **Secondary Button:** Filled background in muted sage green tint (`rgba(165, 175, 121, 0.18)`), deep olive text (`#454B2A`), no border. Hover: sage tint expands to 28% opacity.
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, 1px border (`#D5CFC5`), warm peat text (`#2C2621`). Hover: surface fills with warm stone (`#F5F2ED`).

### 2. Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#D5CFC5`, radius `rounded` (8px). Inner text `#2C2621`, placeholder `#9A948C`. Focus: border switches to primary brown (`#8A5F41`) paired with an ambient 3px warm silt glow. Error: border transitions to terracotta-crimson (`#B94A38`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px boxes, 1.5px border (`#D5CFC5`), radius 5px (checkbox) or circular (radio). When checked: fills with `#8A5F41` displaying a crisp white micro icon.

### 3. Cards & Lists
- **Standard Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px perimeter border `#E5DFD6`, radius `rounded-lg` (16px), padding `space-lg` (24px). Elevation follows Level 1 ambient umber shadow. Hover states slightly lift with Level 2 elevation and border transition to `#D5CFC5`.
- **List Groups:** Divided by hairline rules (`#E5DFD6`). Row hover introduces a faint backdrop of `#F5F2ED` with `space-sm` rounded internal padding.

### 4. Chips & Tags
- **Category Chips:** Height 28px, radius `9999px`, font `label-md`. Background `#EEEAE2`, text `#5E5953`.
- **Botanical / Olive Sage Badges:** Background `rgba(165, 175, 121, 0.22)`, text `#3D4323`, subtle border in `rgba(165, 175, 121, 0.4)`. Used for positive status, nature certifications, and active filters.

### 5. Specialized Grounded Components
- **Architectural Banner / Stat Tiles:** Tonal container filled in light travertine (`#F5F2ED`), displaying large `display-lg` numbers in `#8A5F41` balanced by uppercase `label-sm` subtitles in weathered stone.
- **Divider Strata:** Thick 2px natural accent dividers rendered in `#A5AF79` at 40% opacity to visually partition major editorial sections without visual harshness.
